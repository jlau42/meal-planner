# 3 Chicken & Veggie Dinners (Family of 4)

Household size: 4

## Day 1
- **Dinner**: Sheet-Pan Chicken, Potatoes & Green Beans (40 min) x4
- _Per person: 520 kcal, 40g protein, 42g carbs, 20g fat_

## Day 2
- **Dinner**: Lemon Chicken Quinoa Bowl (30 min) x4
- _Per person: 520 kcal, 42g protein, 44g carbs, 18g fat_

## Day 3
- **Dinner**: Coconut Chicken Curry (40 min) x4
- _Per person: 580 kcal, 36g protein, 48g carbs, 26g fat_

## Prep notes

Buy chicken in one trip: ~1.5 lb thighs for Day 1, chicken breast for Day 2, ~1.5 lb thighs for Day 3. Lemons are used Day 1 and Day 2 - buy 2-3. Day 2 bowl can be prepped ahead (cook quinoa and chicken Day 1 while the sheet pan roasts). Day 3 curry: keep curry paste light for the kids and serve extra rice on the side; leftovers reheat well for lunches.

## Cook list

- Sheet-Pan Chicken, Potatoes & Green Beans: 1 batch(es) = 4 servings, 4 planned
- Lemon Chicken Quinoa Bowl: 1 batch(es) = 4 servings, 4 planned
- Coconut Chicken Curry: 1 batch(es) = 4 servings, 4 planned
